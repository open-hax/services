Gates of Truth

Gates of Truth is a simulated universe written in Clojure. You begin as a quantum spark watching a stellar nebula collapse into a star system, and the same world continues from there, cooling and becoming more articulate, into geology, ecology, and eventually civilisation.

The rule that matters most is that there is a single simulation substrate. Everything that exists in the world exists in one place, under one set of rules, rather than as several parallel systems that have to be kept in agreement. Systems are pure and operate in defined phases, and a given piece of state has exactly one writer.

That constraint sounds academic until you have watched a simulation where it does not hold. Two systems that both believe they own a value do not produce a bug you can find; they produce a world that is subtly wrong in a way nobody can reproduce.

Gates of Truth is where the constellation's ideas about purity, ownership and phase discipline get tested against a problem large enough to punish getting them wrong.
