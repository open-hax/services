# Open Hax Promethean Garden

Open Hax is a garden for tools, research, art, and systems that make their contracts visible.

Content is authored once. Garden contracts decide membership and languages. Publication contracts decide placement. View contracts decide presentation. Translation receipts and human approvals decide which localized bytes are allowed to become public.

This separation keeps a sentence from becoming a layout rule, keeps a theme from becoming content authority, and lets every translation be reviewed before it reaches the public site.
