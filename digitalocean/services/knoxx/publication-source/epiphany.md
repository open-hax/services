Epiphany

Epiphany is a knowledge archaeology system. It turns long-lived repositories of notes and documentation into a searchable environment that preserves evidence rather than discarding it.

The distinction it insists on is between finding a passage and understanding its status. Ordinary search finds exact words. Retrieval systems built on embeddings find plausible passages, and will happily hand you a confident paragraph without telling you it was abandoned two years ago, contradicted later, or written as a question rather than a conclusion.

Epiphany is built for the other question. How did this idea emerge, how did it change, where did it diverge, and what caused it to be reconsidered. Because it is backed by version history, those questions have answers that can be inspected rather than asserted.

The purpose is to find what you already thought, look at the evidence for it, trace how it evolved, and then decide what is actually worth researching next.
