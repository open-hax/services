OpenHax builds tools, research, art, and systems that make their contracts visible. That phrase is not decoration. Almost everything here is organised around the same idea: the rules a system runs by should be written down as data, in the open, where a person can read them and a machine can check them.

This garden is the reading surface for that work. Each document below introduces one piece of the constellation, what problem it exists to solve, and how it relates to its neighbours. You can read them in any order. If you want the shortest path to understanding how the whole thing hangs together, read The Constellation next.

Everything on this site exists in five languages. The English text is authored by a person. The other four are produced by a translation agent, reviewed by a person against the specific revision it was produced from, and only then published. No translated sentence reaches this page without a human having approved that exact revision of it.

That arrangement is itself one of the systems described here. If you want to see it from the inside, read How This Page Got Here.
