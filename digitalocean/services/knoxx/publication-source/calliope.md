Calliope

Calliope is the creative corpus. It holds lyrics, prompts, metadata, artwork, audio renders, classifications, and the record of creative decisions as they were actually made.

Most systems that touch creative work throw away the process and keep the result. Calliope keeps both, and treats the process as the more interesting half. What was tried, what was rejected, what a piece was before it became what it is: these are evidence, and evidence is append-only here.

The music on this site comes from Calliope. What you hear is a selection from a much larger body of work, chosen because it was marked as worth keeping rather than merely produced.

Calliope also participates in the wider ingestion and knowledge machinery. A corpus that only a person can navigate stops being useful at about the size Calliope passed some time ago.
