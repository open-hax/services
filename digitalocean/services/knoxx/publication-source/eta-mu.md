eta-mu

eta-mu is the agent runtime and workflow repository, and the home of the board that the rest of the work is planned on.

Its name comes from the two operators it is built around. Eta is transduction, the act of consuming an artifact and producing another. Mu is evaluation, the act of judging what came out against what was wanted. A great deal of agent work is those two steps repeated with discipline.

The board lives here too. Work is tracked as cards with explicit states and explicit transitions, so that the question of what is in flight is answerable from the repository rather than from memory. The states are a finite machine, not a set of adjectives, and the transitions between them are checked.

eta-mu also supplies the review machinery that guards the other repositories. When a change is proposed, evidence is gathered, findings are raised, and a gate refuses to let a change through while findings are unresolved. That is a deliberately annoying arrangement. It exists because the alternative is a review that everyone agrees was thorough and nobody can point at.
